About me

![My portrait](4d577e10b5bab3fb1792bc698d8a6e03)

Ivan Bogachev, aka sfi0zy.

Hi. I'm Ivan. For many years, I was a mathematician and creative frontend developer, working with generative art, experimental websites, and routinely turning various "unrealizable" fantasies of our designers into production-ready code.

With time, my role shifted towards data and systems analysis, and at some moment I combined all my experience with a personal interest in psychology and psychiatry to build a mechanical model of the psyche.

This initiative became a long-term interdisciplinary project with the final goal to understand how we operate, from the lowest levels of chemical processes in our brains, to consciousness and patterns of behavior, and to the level of societies and civilizations.

In 2025, the first important part of this project was published as an evolutionary theory of behavior.

We still have a lot of work to do, and I'm not even sure if we have enough tools to finish everything in the near future, but I believe that this project can bring new approaches to the field and help us with resolving the theory crisis in psychology.
